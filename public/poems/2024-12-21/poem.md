# The chocolate factory

There’s a chocolate factory up on the hill,
the entrance is open for those who feel.
No mimicking, no pretending,
If you’re really feeling it, keep on ascending.

The stairs are graded from one to who knows,
the end is an illusion that only time can blow.
There are candies and cookies all around the place,
only accessible if I see tenderness in your face.

The factory has a variety of flavors and machines of all kinds,
the taste of a single piece will stimulate your mind.
From the usual sweetness to the rarity of saltiness,
prepare your palate, you’ll enjoy the altiness.

I’m the chocolatier, who tempers and molds,
enjoying the view and wondering what the future holds.
Crafting chocolate requires patience,
the final product must glare with high radiance.
