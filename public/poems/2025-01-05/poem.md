# Dancing to my music

My conscious mind points at you at times,
sending a signal of hope and good vibes.
The farther time takes us, the weaker the connection,
therefore the hope, for my signal’s reception.

From a materialized dream to one that rarely visits me on my sleep,
and even on such state, the energy flows through me to the beats.
Bump bump, bump bump,
my heart dances to the drums.

“I hope he’s doing well”,
sometimes I think to myself.
I keep on my journey, following my path,
dancing to my music, feeding my own heart.

How odd is this silence after dancing to the sounds,
to peacefully walk after months above the ground.
There is calm, there is me,
I can finally enjoy the sea.
