# Do you…?

Do you play my keys for hearing the melody,
or because I’m just your type?
Do you take me as a medicine,
or just to get dopamine high?

Do you want me in your future,
or is it just to escape the night?
Is it true we’ll meet new cultures,
or am I just accumulating sighs?

Do you close your eyes thinking of me,
or just to imagine someone else?
Am I the one boosting your dreams,
or do you new horizons delve?

Does this mean we’re far from each other,
even though I’m next to you?
Is the magic really over,
and has our love faded to blue?
