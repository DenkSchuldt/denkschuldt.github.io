# Call me by your love

How strange is to feel in the uncertain.
A poem written by Denny K. Schuldt.

***

Keep me in your thoughts,
close to your heart.
Fill me with hugs,
Glue all of my parts.

Call me by your love,
don’t do it by my name.
I seek feeling at home,
so please shorten this space.

Say the unsaid,
just cast the spell.
Mean all the words,
this is a new tale.
