# My Bible

As it is written, shall it be done.

***

Wind is the only witness
to the sighs I’ve stolen.
Although air from my lungs returned,
nothing on earth is forgotten.

The silence as an archive,
my body loses count.
Ephemeral moments yet still present,
I’m complete, but not a whole.

Days are nights and nights are days,
countless hours in this land.
Skeptic of the natural gesture,
the softened touch of another hand.

How do you move on from what’s moved?
Why is time a timeless cycle?
Space and time are always curved,
These poems and verses are my bible.
