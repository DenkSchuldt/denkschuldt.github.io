# A dead past haunted

How do you declare a place… dead?

***

I’m towns away but I can smell it,
dust on surfaces and mites just growing.
A place I no longer want to visit,
but memories alone are going.

Afternoons of tv and laughs,
faded to a foreign land.
The very space that once protected me,
deprived of the warmest hand.

A dead past haunted by the living,
an echo chamber of what it was.
The host for a new micro-universe,
the grieving scene for life’s law.

It will never be brought back to life,
a museum trapped on its own.
Handwritten notes left on the table,
extinguished fire on the stove.
