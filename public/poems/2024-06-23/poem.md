# To the brightest star

Please come back and hug my being,
at least in the form of a beautiful dream.
It’ll comfort me even when profoundly sleeping,
I miss you and I don’t want more weeping.

Losing you aches the deepest of my heart,
you’ve gone from one big caring heart to the brightest star.
Time has its rules and you needed to rest,
following your legacy is what we’ll do best.
