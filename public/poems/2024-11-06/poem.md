# Inside the body of a software guy

I’m a Poet blessed inside the body of a software guy,
numbers and structural thinking have been the pillars of my life.
And yet, one side of my brain enjoys logical challenges,
while the other loves expressing feelings whenever there is silence.

The art of coding is like playing with lego pieces,
you build and build with lines until production releases.
Writing poetry is similar but feels different,
you build verses with words until you form something significant.

I’m really humbled to be able to do both,
they feed my curiosity and soul as I grow.
I only ask the universe for wisdom and more time,
between code within apps, and verses with rhymes.
