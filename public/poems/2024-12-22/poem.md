# A sublime experience

Love is so beautiful that it is unfair to lock it in your chest,
or even worse to make it cold and calculated like a game of chess.
You see, love is such a beautiful thing to grow inside,
to nurture and take care of for its rewards in life.

What is love? You may wonder yourself.
Not a magical formula that you can use on an excel cell.
Maybe it’s a state of mind, or a way of living,
or maybe just a chemical reaction felt for us still breathing.

Some physics compare love to the fundamental forces,
Gravity, electromagnetism, but only love human connections forges.
Some poets think about love as a sublime experience to live.
Ever changing and timeless, something you can receive and give.

Love can transform, from zero to a hundred,
love can make you feel at your best, but never haunted.
If any, that wouldn’t be love anymore…
Would be something else, something shattered on the floor.
