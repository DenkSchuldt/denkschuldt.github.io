# I loved so very deep

I wrote so many things,
that may never see the light.
I loved so very deep,
that it almost got me blind.

Memories come and go,
like a highway in my mind.
Even hours and some sounds,
on important days and nights.

I remember, I remember…
how I tried until surrendered.
The road was lonely and all so gray,
to walk alone, the price to pay.
