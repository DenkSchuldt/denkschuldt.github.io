# Safebox

Mutual, that’s the word. That’s the key to unravel my world.

***

In a world of solitude,
let me with you stay.
Truth is you’re not lonely,
It’s just a trick your mind says.

I want to be your safebox,
the one that’ll save your heart.
To share your joys or secrets.
Together, not apart.

I’m offering companionship,
but you’ll have to save mine.
A rare psychic fusion,
my mind to your mind.

My thoughts with you will be safe,
what a beautiful goal.
Aiming for destiny and not fate,
my soul to your soul.
