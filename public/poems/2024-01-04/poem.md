# The strategy of the mystery

Let’s be honest, everybody wants it.
All of them want it to be fantastic.
They all seek for true love,
But at the first sign, they fly like a dove.

They want the feeling to chase them,
They want their bodies to tell them,
But are too afraid to feel it,
Fearing that they may kill it.

That’s why gay dating is so fragile,
And most men lose matches so agile.
The strategy of the mystery,
Never works and end up in misery.

I salute and give honors
To the men that show their true colors.
The brave ones that are authentic,
And have no intentions to fake it.

Those rarities of the city,
Those souls that are still pretty.
Keep shining and shedding light,
For others to follow you and do it right.
